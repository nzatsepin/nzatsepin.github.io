Funding, Scholarships & How to Apply

Zatsepin Lab - eligibility, funding pathways, scholarships, international-student options, and how to apply. See Available Projects for what's on offer.

What to include in your email (after reading this page and the Swinburne website):
  • Which project you're interested in, and why
  • CV
  • Academic transcript
  • Relevant skills (see Available Projects for what to mention)
  • Any prior research (including specific instrumentation, programming, or software) or relevant work experience
This page is a quick-reference guide only. Swinburne's scholarship terms, dates and requirements change - always check the official pages linked below before you rely on anything here.
Apply early. Swinburne's scholarship rounds are highly competitive, and the university expects supervisors to put forward their strongest candidate rather than several - so I can only support one applicant per round for a Swinburne-competitive scholarship (SUPRA/RTPS). I need time to review and rank candidates well ahead of the round deadline, so get your eligibility checked, documents ready, and email sent to me as early as possible.

Our own lab-funded PhD scholarships (ARC-funded): offers made, pending enrolment.

The process, in short

1

Check the project list

Browse Available Projects and identify which one(s) interest you and why.

2

Check your eligibility

Entry requirements differ by degree (PhD, Masters by Research, Honours). See Swinburne's entry requirements page.

3

Prepare your email

See what to include above.

4

Email us

Send the above to nzatsepin at swin edu au. We'll follow up - this usually includes a short interview before we agree to supervise.

We receive many enquiries, including from backgrounds that don't match our projects. We can't personally reply to every email despite our best efforts - but we do read them all, and we interview our top candidates. A specific email that shows you've read the project descriptions goes a long way; please don't send mass-produced or AI-generated enquiries, as these are easy to spot and don't help your application.

5

Formal application & scholarship (once we've agreed to supervise)

You'll submit the university's HDR Research Proposal Template with your online application, and - if applying for a scholarship - arrange two referee reports (forms below). Full process: Swinburne - How to apply for a research degree.

Referee forms (needed only at Step 5, once you're applying for a scholarship): Academic referee report form · Non-academic referee report form (at least 2 referees; academic preferred where possible).

Review time: the university's review process usually takes up to 8 weeks. It can take longer if your application is incomplete or needs extra information.

Understanding Swinburne scholarships

You submit one application - "Swinburne Research Scholarships" - and are automatically considered for both SUPRA and RTPS. You don't choose between them. Stipend value for both: $35,500/yr (2026 rate), 3 years + possible 6-month extension (Swinburne terms). This is competitive and requires a full research proposal (your supervisor helps write it) with strong alignment to Swinburne's research strategy and priority areas - worth reading before you apply.

If you win RTPS: domestic students get tuition automatically covered by a separate scholarship, RTP Fees-Offset. International students: tuition is NOT automatic - it requires a separate Tuition Fee Scholarship application, case-by-case, needing your supervisor to submit a justification approved by the School and DVC(R). Please check this directly with Swinburne - talk to us early so we can factor this into your application timeline.

This also applies if your stipend is externally/lab-funded (e.g. ARC), not from Swinburne or a government scheme. A stipend from us doesn't cover tuition - international students still need the separate Tuition Fee Scholarship application above, with our support, or tuition fees are payable. Please raise this with us as early as possible if you're an international applicant.

SUPRA vs RTPS: the site doesn't spell out a strategic difference beyond funding source - apply once, you're assessed for both.

2027 round dates are not yet published - check the SRSS page for current dates before applying. Minimum academic threshold stated by Swinburne: Bachelor Degree with First Class Honours (or equivalent) - this is also our minimum for direct lab consideration.

Duration: the Commonwealth RTP scheme allows up to 4 years for a full-time PhD and up to 2 years for a Masters; Swinburne's stipend scholarships run 3 years with a possible 6-month extension. Background on the Commonwealth scheme: education.gov.au RTP FAQ.

Honours project selection has no fixed university-wide deadline - it's supervisor-driven, so get in touch early. General admission info: Bachelor of Science (Honours).

Other Swinburne scholarships

Swinburne runs a wide range of scholarships for students facing unique circumstances - financial hardship, disability, regional/remote background, and more - some open to undergrad, some postgrad, some both. Browse Swinburne's full scholarships list and follow whichever look relevant to your situation.

Tip: the list is large - search or filter by keywords relevant to you (e.g. "women", "disability", "regional", "Indigenous", "STEM") rather than scrolling the whole list. If something looks like a top-up (stacks on top of your PhD scholarship, doesn't replace it), you can apply for it after your PhD/scholarship application is underway and you've confirmed your own eligibility - no need to ask us first.

Georgina Sweet Fellowship (Australian Graduate Women) - for women who are Australian citizens or permanent residents, enrolled in a PhD by research in STEMM, in the second or a later year. Funds up to one year of extra project funding to extend the scope of your PhD. Check the current round and value: AGW Fellowships.

International student pathways

Swinburne Partnered PhD - jointly enrolled and supervised at Swinburne and one international partner institution, with at least two supervisors (one at each) and up to one year spent in Australia. Current partners: IIT Hyderabad, IIT Madras, KMUTT (Thailand), Mahidol (Thailand), University of Bayreuth (Germany), University of Malaya (Malaysia), University of Stuttgart (Germany). Entry requirements and how to apply: HDRINTPartnerships@swinburne.edu.au.

Swinburne Offshore PhD - enrol at Swinburne Sarawak (Malaysia) or BRIN (Indonesia), spending up to one year in Australia. Details: Offshore PhD programs.

USA-Australia exchange - funds Masters and PhD students moving between the USA and Australia in either direction: American Australian Association.

DAAD - support for Australian students undertaking research in Germany: DAAD Australia.

International applicants: visas

Visa processing can be more difficult from regions of political instability. For official visa and application guidance, contact Swinburne directly: hdrscholarships@swin.edu.au or see Support for International Students.
