Research Summary

Publications: 62 refereed journal papers; over 10,500 citations; h-index 39; 27 papers with 100+ citations. Journals include Nature (4), Science (5), Cell (1), Nature Communications (9), PNAS (4), JACS (1); 2 invited review articles; 1 sole-authored invited book chapter. Full list: Google Scholar. 27 papers cited in 32 patents (source: lens.org)

Research: Serial femtosecond crystallography (SFX) at X-ray free-electron lasers (XFELs) and serial synchrotron crystallography: algorithm and software development, experiment design, and application to macromolecular targets and hybrid materials. Focus on time-resolved crystallography, radiation damage, and drug-relevant targets including membrane proteins and bacterial enzymes. Current focus: time-resolved serial crystallography at the Australian Synchrotron for antimicrobial-resistance and neurodegeneration targets; fluctuation X-ray scattering nanocrystallography.

Key contributions:

  • Corresponding senior author, first membrane protein structure from a megahertz-rate XFEL (Nature Communications 2019; Photosystem I, ~1 MDa).
  • Established SFX in lipidic cubic phase (LCP-SFX) as a robust method for room-temperature GPCR structure determination; 10 LCP-SFX publications.
  • First time-resolved SFX molecular movies at atomic resolution (Science 2014, 2016); first mix-and-inject serial crystallography (Nature 2017).
  • Proof-of-concept serial microcrystallography at five synchrotron beamlines (ESRF, APS ×2, ALBA, NSLS-II); currently establishing SSX at the Australian Synchrotron MX3.
  • Head of XFEL Crystallography Data Analysis, NSF BioXFEL Science and Technology Center (2013–2019); Chief Investigator or co-CI on over 100 XFEL experiments.
